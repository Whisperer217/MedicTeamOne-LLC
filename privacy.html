Privacy Policy

Medic Team One, LLC

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide when requesting training or contacting us, including your name, email address, optional phone number, selected training option, preferred timing, group size, location details, and any other information you choose to include. If training is confirmed, we may separately collect information needed for waivers, records, or payment.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, propose and confirm training arrangements, communicate about a session, process an agreed payment, and maintain appropriate business and training records. We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The training inquiry form is processed by Formspree, which receives the information submitted through that form so it can be delivered to Medic Team One. If payment is arranged, the applicable payment provider will process payment information under its own privacy policy. Medic Team One does not receive or store a full payment-card number through the website inquiry form.

4. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by Medic Team One by contacting us at jacob@medicteamone.com. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, safety, or legitimate business purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
