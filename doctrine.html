Field Comparison

Civilian Responder One: the difference is readiness.

A critical incident in the outdoors lasts minutes. Professional help is often hours away. The gap is you.

Falls from Tree Stands & Slips on Deck

The #1 cause of serious injury. Leads to broken bones, spinal damage, and internal bleeding.

Firearm & Broadhead Accidents

Low probability, but catastrophic consequences. Causes massive, life-ending hemorrhage in minutes.

Environmental Exposure

Drowning, hypothermia, and heat stroke are silent, constant threats that can incapacitate and kill.

Before Training

You are a spectator to a tragedy — forced to wait, helpless, for professionals who may arrive too late. Your actions, however well-intentioned, are likely to be chaotic and ineffective.

After Training

You become an effective first responder — with the clarity to ignore distractions and the confidence to execute life-saving skills in the correct order.

MEDIC TEAM ONE

Bridging battlefield medicine and civilian preparedness. Montgomery County, Texas.

Civilian Responder One (Basic) is a preparedness training program, not a state or nationally recognized EMS certification. Participation does not authorize attendees to represent themselves as licensed medical providers. Under Texas law (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §74.151 — Good Samaritan Act), individuals who in good faith provide emergency aid are protected from liability except in cases of willful or wanton negligence.
