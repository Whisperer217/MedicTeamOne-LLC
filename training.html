Scheduling

Medic Team One does not run on a fixed public class calendar. Training dates are created around actual client requirements — individual, group, workplace, or custom.

When a scheduled session has capacity to spare, additional participants may be accommodated where appropriate. This is a flexible training logistics model, not a traditional scheduled-course catalog.

Location is Montgomery County, TX — specific venue provided upon scheduling.

Bridging battlefield medicine and civilian preparedness. Montgomery County, Texas.

Civilian Responder One (Basic) is a preparedness training program, not a state or nationally recognized EMS certification. Participation does not authorize attendees to represent themselves as licensed medical providers. Under Texas law (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §74.151 — Good Samaritan Act), individuals who in good faith provide emergency aid are protected from liability except in cases of willful or wanton negligence.
